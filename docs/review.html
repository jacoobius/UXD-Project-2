    A LITTLE REFLECTION

    Your AI coach

    Qwen 2.5 runs inside your browser on this device’s graphics chip. Free, no account, and your journal never leaves this device. The first reflection downloads about 1 GB of model files; later visits reuse the browser’s copy.

    AI feedback can be mistaken. Use the calculated figures as your reference. Browsers without WebGPU get a calculated reflection instead. This is general fitness feedback, not medical advice.

    Exercise by exercise

    Best set this week against your last session

    Different weights are compared by estimated one-rep max (Epley formula, most reliable up to 12–15 reps). At the same weight, more reps count as progress. Volume is sets × reps × weight, or total reps for bodyweight exercises. Technique, effort, and range of motion are not measured.
